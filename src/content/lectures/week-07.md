Storage is specified with two independent numbers, and most conversations
about it use one. This lecture separates them and then shows a case where the
distinction decides the outcome.

## Outline

- power and energy as separate constraints, with separate units
- duration as the ratio between them
- an illustrative specification brought to a multi-day event
- which of the four available levers actually changes the result
- why storage procured for a daily peak can be irrelevant to a drought
