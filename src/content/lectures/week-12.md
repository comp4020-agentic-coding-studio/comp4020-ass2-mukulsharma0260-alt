The last lecture is about a sentence: the one naming the event your system
does not survive.

## Outline

- why a declared failure boundary is an engineering output, not a disclaimer
- what makes a boundary checkable rather than rhetorical
- how a boundary stated in your own parameters can be tested by someone else
- what [Design for the Worst Week](/assessments/a4-design-for-the-worst-week/)
  is actually marking

A design that names only what it survives has answered half the question.
