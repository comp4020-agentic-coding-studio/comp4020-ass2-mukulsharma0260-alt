An annual renewable share is a summary statistic, and summary statistics are
chosen. The same system that reports a comfortable figure across a year
contains hours where output is nearly absent, and no average is obliged to
mention them.

## How this advances the thesis

It establishes the gap the rest of the course lives in. If averages and hours
told the same story there would be no course: you would size a system to its
mean and be finished. Week 1 shows the two views of one system side by side so
the disagreement is visible before anything is built on it.

## In the session

We take the course's synthetic fortnight and look at it twice — once as a
single number, once hour by hour. Then you go looking for the worst contiguous
window in the trace yourself.

The point of the exercise is not the answer. It is noticing that the answer
depends entirely on how long a window you were told to look for.

## What next week needs

You will have found a bad period without having been told what "bad" means.
That is the problem [week 2](/sessions/week-02/) picks up, and it is why
[the event](/the-event/) has to be defined before it can be designed against.
