Week 1 asked you to find the worst period. This week asks what gave you the
right to call it an event at all.

## How this complicates the thesis

The thesis says a defensible design names the event it survives. Week 2 shows
that naming is not a neutral act. Four defensible-looking definitions applied
to the same fortnight produce four different sets of qualifying periods —
sometimes including the empty set. The line is a modelling decision, and it
decides what the rest of the semester is even allowed to see.

## In the session

We classify one fortnight four ways, changing threshold, duration, geographic
scope and which sources count. You watch periods appear and disappear from the
qualifying set without the weather changing at all.
