Up to this point every shortfall has had a technical answer. This week there
is not enough, no further technical move exists, and the question becomes who
goes without.

## How this challenges the thesis

The thesis asks a design to state what it survives. Week 9 asks who "it"
refers to. A system that stays within its frequency limits by shedding a
suburb has survived in one sense and failed in another, and the engineering
description of the event does not settle which. Load shedding is where the
design's values stop being implicit.

## In the session

Fixed available generation, more load than generation, and a set of competing
demands. You allocate. The board records what you chose to sacrifice, and the
discussion afterwards is about the ordering you used and whether you would
defend it in public.
