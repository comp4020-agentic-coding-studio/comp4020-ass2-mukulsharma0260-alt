The same blackout can be a generation failure, a transmission failure, a market
design failure, a governance failure or a weather event, depending on where the
author drew the edge of the system.

## How this challenges the thesis

The thesis treats the failure boundary as something an engineer states. Week 11
argues the boundary is also chosen, and often chosen by whoever is writing
afterwards. That does not make it arbitrary — but it means attribution is
partly a question about institutions, and a design that names its own boundary
is making an institutional claim as well as a technical one.

## In the session

One fictional failure, five reports of it, each written from a different
vantage point. You locate the system boundary each report assumes, then write
the sixth report yourself and declare your own boundary rather than letting it
be inferred.
