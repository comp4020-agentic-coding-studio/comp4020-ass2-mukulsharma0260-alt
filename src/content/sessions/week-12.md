The semester closes where it began, with the configuration you nominated in
week 1 — now with eleven weeks of reasons to change it, and an obligation to
say what it still cannot survive.

## How this advances the thesis

This is the thesis, stated as a deliverable. A design that names only what it
survives has answered half the question, and the half it skipped is the half
that tells a reader whether to trust the first. Week 12 treats the declared
failure boundary as the primary engineering output rather than a caveat
attached to one.

## In the session

Studio critique. You present a system, the event it survives, and the event
that beats it. The room's job is to test whether those three statements are
consistent with each other and precise enough to be checked.

There is deliberately no correct system. There are systems whose limits are
stated, and systems whose limits will be discovered by someone else later.

## Its relationship to the canonical event

[The event](/the-event/) gave the cohort a shared starting definition so that
twelve weeks of argument could be about systems rather than vocabulary. By now
you should be able to say precisely how your own definition differs from it,
and why.
