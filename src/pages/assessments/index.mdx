Four tasks, one cumulative project, 100% in total. Each stage takes the
previous stage's output as its input, so nothing here can be attempted in
isolation — and a weakness you leave in place early will be operating your
system by week 11.

Every task is bounded by the definition at [the event](/the-event/), or by the
definition you argue for in its place.
